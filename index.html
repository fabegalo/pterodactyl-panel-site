Live console

The server's history loads the moment you open the tab, and new output arrives as it happens. Type a command and read the answer in the same place.

Dashboard

Power state, CPU, memory and disk against your plan's limits. Minecraft servers also show players online, version and ping.

File manager

Browse folders, open configuration files in a built-in editor, rename, delete, create folders, and copy a signed download link.

Backups

Create, list and delete backups. Locked backups are marked, so you can tell what your panel's retention policy will not touch.

Several servers, one app

Switch servers from the dashboard in one tap. Administrator accounts can list every server on the panel, not only the ones they own.

Signing in

Two ways in. Pick whichever you are comfortable with.

Recommended Your panel account

  1. Type your panel address.
  2. Sign in on your panel's own login page, inside the app.
  3. The app creates its API key for you and remembers it.

The login page is your panel's, not a copy — so two-factor authentication, Cloudflare and SSO keep working. Your password is never seen by the app.

An API key you made yourself

  1. Create a key under Account → API Credentials in your panel.
  2. Type your panel address in the app.
  3. Open “Use an API key” and paste it.

Kept for anyone who would rather not type a password into an app, or who wants to manage the key by hand.

The address can be a domain or an IP, with or without a scheme or port — panel.example.com, https://panel.example.com, 192.168.0.10, http://192.168.0.10:8080. The app works out the rest.

Questions people actually ask

Do I have to give the app my panel password?

No. The app opens your panel's real login page and never reads what you type into it. What it keeps afterwards is an API key, stored in the phone's encrypted keystore.

Does it work with a self-hosted panel on my home network?

Yes, as long as your phone can reach it. Plain http:// addresses and custom ports are supported.

Is it only for Minecraft?

No. Anything Pterodactyl hosts works: console, files, backups and power controls are the same everywhere. The extra card with players and ping only appears for Minecraft servers, because that is where the query protocol exists.

I paid to remove ads and reinstalled. Do I pay again?

No. The purchase is tied to your Google account. Reinstall, open the app, and it restores itself; there is also a restore button on the Remove Ads screen.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet. The app is Android only for now.

Privacy
